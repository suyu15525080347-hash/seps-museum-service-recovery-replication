Data availability

All empirical source data used in this study are publicly available official statistics. Museum-performance data are from the UK Department for Culture, Media and Sport (DCMS), *DCMS-sponsored Museums and Galleries Annual Performance Indicators 2024/25*. Grant-in-Aid data are from DCMS, *Total Income of DCMS-funded cultural organisations 2023/24 and 2024/25*. The general-price deflator is from HM Treasury, *GDP deflators at market prices, and money GDP September 2026 (Quarterly National Accounts)*. The National Audit Office report, *The financial resilience of DCMS-sponsored museums and galleries*, is used for external comparison.

The processed analysis tables, code, robustness checks, figure inputs and GIS provenance files are publicly available at https://github.com/suyu15525080347-hash/seps-museum-service-recovery-replication . The National Audit Office report is accessible from its official publisher rather than mirrored in the repository because of separate reproduction restrictions. Geographic layers retain their original Open Government Licence or OpenStreetMap ODbL attribution and reuse conditions, as appropriate. This GitHub repository has no dataset DOI at present.
